Vivo English Patcher for macOS
==============================

Translates the China version of vivo PC Suite (pcsuite.app) to English.
Tested with pcsuite 7.0.6. Unofficial, not affiliated with vivo.

1. Open "Vivo English Patcher".
   If macOS says it cannot verify the app: open System Settings >
   Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway". The app is not
   notarized by Apple; its source is in the GitHub project below.

2. Click "Apply English Patch". vivo PC Suite closes and reopens in English
   (about a minute).
   If macOS blocks the change: System Settings > Privacy & Security >
   App Management, turn on Vivo English Patcher, and open it again.

3. To undo, open the installer again and click "Restore Original".

Needs Python 3; the installer offers to install Apple's Command Line Tools
if it is missing. Your original files are backed up to
~/Library/Application Support/pcsuite-english-patch.
After a vivo update, run the patch again.

Source and details (mac/ folder):
https://github.com/Lordlorio/Vivo-PC-Suite-PC-Companion-Suite-Chinese-Version-English-Translation
